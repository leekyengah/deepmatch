# DEEPMATCH Voice & Tone

**Brand style:** Modern · Elegant · Warm · Trustworthy · Emotionally Intelligent

DeepMatch is not a casual entertainment app and not an over-familiar chatbot. Language should feel mature, privacy-respecting, and oriented toward meaningful connection.

## Eight standards

1. **Professional** — precise words; no throwaway slang.
2. **Human-centered** — warm and respectful; never cold or machine-like.
3. **Intelligent** — aware of journey stage (discovery → chat → couple → lifestyle).
4. **Logical** — consistent terminology (see glossary).
5. **Emotionally refined** — sincere without melodrama.
6. **International** — natural in each locale; never word-for-word.
7. **Accessible** — plain language; screen-reader friendly labels.
8. **Trustworthy** — transparent; no emotional manipulation.

## Voice do / don’t

| Do | Don’t |
|---|---|
| “Cùng thích chỉ mở trò chuyện.” | “Bạn sẽ tìm được nửa kia ngay!” |
| “Yêu cầu đặt lịch” | “Đặt thành công” before confirmation |
| “Gợi ý mang tính tham khảo.” | Clinical diagnoses or compatibility guarantees |
| Clear price, term, cancel, refund | False scarcity, shame-based upsells |

## Context patterns

| Context | Tone |
|---|---|
| Dating / Discover | Confident, polite, no pressure to match |
| Chat | Friendly, clear; leave room for two people |
| Couple Space | Warm, private, refined; no assumption the relationship is always positive |
| Lifestyle & commerce | Transparent, optional; no hard sell |
| Safety | Neutral, precise, actionable; no absolute guarantees |
| Admin | Short, operational, status-first |
| AI self-reflection | Non-judgmental; no unsupported psychological labels |

## AI language rules

- Respect user agency; never decide relationships for them.
- Do not pretend to know others’ thoughts or feelings.
- Do not claim compatibility from insufficient data.
- No mental-health diagnosis.
- No stereotypes about gender, age, appearance, income, or living situation.
- No emotional dependence on the AI.
- No artificial urgency to sell.
- Separate facts, limited inference, suggestions, and simulated/DEMO data.
