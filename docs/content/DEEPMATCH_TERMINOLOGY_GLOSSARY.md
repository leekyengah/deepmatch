## Do not translate

- Brand: DeepMatch, DeepSafe, DeepMind
- Labels: Premium, DEMO, SANDBOX_ONLY, Staging
- Technical: feature flag names, error codes (`UNAUTHENTICATED`, …), SKUs, IDs
- User-generated content (bios, messages, journals) unless the user explicitly requests translation

## Legal / sensitive

Terms, privacy policy, mental-health adjacent AI copy, refund and cancellation notices require **HUMAN_REVIEW_REQUIRED** before production legal sign-off. UI staging may ship pending review.
